Our Story
View the Cinemat​ic Trailer

In a cinematic journey that unfolds like an intimate memoir,

"Pursuit of Equality" takes viewers behind the curtain, providing an exclusive behind-the-scenes look at the groundbreaking events that shook San Francisco in 2004. Mayor Gavin Newsom's daring move to issue same-sex marriage licenses, a pivotal moment captured by a film crew with unprecedented access, set the stage for a cultural revolution.

As the documentary revisits covert moments before the media storm,

viewers are granted access to Mayor Newsom's senior staff, the silent architects orchestrating the nation's first same-sex couple's vows. This is not merely a celebration of love; it's an unfiltered, behind-the-scenes exploration of a historical event that would echo through time.

Beyond the bureaucratic maneuvers, the film crew takes audiences through city streets,

courtrooms, and the grand steps of City Hall. It's a raw, unscripted immersion into the gradual evolution of hearts and minds, showcasing the resilience of those who dared to love in the face of societal opposition.

Within the halls of San Francisco's City Hall, certain biased groups actively work to obstruct and shut down same-sex marriages.

 Driven by discriminatory motives, these groups aim to hinder the city's progress toward achieving equality. Despite their efforts, advocates and leaders within San Francisco remain steadfast in their commitment to fostering inclusivity. The city continues to stand as a symbol of progress in the ongoing struggle for equal rights, undeterred by the opposition that seeks to undermine the fundamental principle of equality for all citizens, regardless of sexual orientation.

Navigating the legal twists—the California Supreme Court's 2008 decision,

the passage of Proposition 8, and the consequential rulings of the U.S. Supreme Court in 2013 and 2015. This documentary serves as a living, breathing testament, documenting triumphs and setbacks in the relentless pursuit of equality.

Now, two decades after its creation, this behind-the-scenes gem stands not only as

a witness to history but as a powerful reminder of the progress achieved. "Pursuit of Equality" is more than a documentary; it's a time capsule of change, a celebration of love, and an exclusive journey that unfolds with the authenticity that only behind-the-scenes access can provide.
